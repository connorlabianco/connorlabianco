## Featured Projects

### [StudyPilot](https://github.com/connorlabianco/StudyPilot): a study coach built into Canvas

A free Chrome extension, published on the Chrome Web Store, that turns Canvas courses into a study plan. It needs no account, API key, or server, and works with the AI tools students already use.

- **Weekly overview:** deadlines across courses, what changed since your last visit, and exam dates pulled from syllabi and announcements
- **Lecture mastery:** breaks each lecture into checklist items, then quizzes you with self-graded questions, prioritizing what you missed
- **Planning:** sizes assignments, fits them into your free study time, and exports or syncs to Google Calendar
- **Extras:** JupyterHub notebook support, cross-course search, grade calculator, focus timer

### [SpeechLab](https://github.com/connorlabianco/SpeechLab): AI feedback on how you speak

Upload a video of yourself speaking and get back an analysis of your delivery. The pipeline extracts the audio, transcribes it, classifies speech emotion across ten categories with Wav2Vec2, measures speaking rate, and uses Gemini to turn the results into coaching feedback. It includes an AI coach chat, timeline charts, PDF reports, and Google sign-in with analysis history. This is the second version of [Speechably](https://github.com/connorlabianco/Speechably), my original hackathon build.
